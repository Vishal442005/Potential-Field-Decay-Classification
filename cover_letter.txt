\vspace{0.6em}
\noindent
\textbf{Context and Core Motivation}:
For over six decades, supervised pattern classification has been dominated by boundary-partitioning paradigms---Support Vector Machines (SVMs), decision tree ensembles (Random Forests, XGBoost, LightGBM), and Deep Neural Networks (DNNs)---which carve Euclidean feature spaces into discrete regions. While empirically successful on traditional benchmarks, these global boundary architectures suffer from foundational vulnerabilities: (1) an absence of local certainty cores, yielding uncalibrated, arbitrarily high confidence far from empirical data; (2) acute sensitivity to small-magnitude adversarial perturbations ($\|\boldsymbol{\delta}\|_2 \ll 1$); and (3) an inability to provide native directional feedback or actionable counterfactual recourse without non-convex post-hoc optimization.

\vspace{0.6em}
\noindent
\textbf{Key Innovations and Scientific Contributions}:
In this paper, we reverse the boundary-fitting orthodoxy by formulating \textbf{Potential Field Decay Classification (PFDC)}, an interpretable machine learning paradigm grounded in physical electrostatics:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Dual-Zone Potential Profile \& Certified Invariance}: Each training observation acts as a localized radial energy source radiating an inner deterministic core zone ($R^{\mathrm{core}}$) of saturated certainty ($1.0$), transitioning into an exponential decay shell ($R^{\mathrm{decay}}$). We theoretically prove a \textbf{Certified Adversarial Invariance Theorem} guaranteeing $100\%$ prediction invariance against $\ell_2$ perturbations within cores, completely depriving gradient adversaries of torque ($\nabla_{\mathbf{x}} \mathcal{L} \equiv \mathbf{0}$).
    \item \textbf{PAC Generalization Bounds}: We establish the formal statistical learning foundations of PFDC, deriving a sharp PAC generalization error bound via empirical Rademacher complexity scaling as $\mathcal{O}(M^{-1/2})$.
    \item \textbf{Limit-Cycle Quenching Optimization}: We derive exact closed-form analytical gradients and formulate an \textbf{Accelerating Exponential Learning-Rate Schedule (Case 3)} that quenches $93.3\%$ of boundary-crossing limit-cycle oscillations with Lyapunov asymptotic stability.
    \item \textbf{Multi-Dimensional Orthogonal Subspace Stacking}: We introduce 2D Overlapped Stacking and Common-Pivot 3D Superposition with learnable anisotropic variable weights $\mathbf{w}$, resolving high-dimensional measure concentration while providing full visual geometric interpretability on real clinical cohorts.
    \item \textbf{Sub-Linear $\mathcal{O}(\log N)$ Spatial Indexing}: Exploiting compact support truncation, spatial Ball-Tree indexing prunes exterior non-interacting clusters, accelerating query evaluation by up to $2.81\times$ over brute force and $3.8\times$ over SVM at $N=10,000$ with exact numerical equivalence ($< 10^{-14}$ error).
    \item \textbf{Continuous Explainable AI (XAI) Recourse}: We prove that PFDC's curl-free force streamlines ($\nabla \times \mathbf{F} = \mathbf{0}$) generate continuous counterfactual recourse trajectories \textbf{5.0$\times$ faster than Wachter's non-convex optimization} with a $100\%$ convergence rate into safety cores, visualized via our proposed Electro-Potential Field-Aligned Projection (EP-FAP).
    \item \textbf{Exhaustive Real-World Benchmarking}: Standardized evaluations across four diverse scientific domains (Financial Security, Biomedical Oncology, Aerospace Radar, Marine Geophysics) and medium-scale tabular benchmarks against TabNet and FT-Transformer (California Housing, $N=20,640$) demonstrate superior certified robustness and competitive predictive accuracy.
\end{enumerate}
